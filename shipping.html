Delivery policy

LAST UPDATED 20 SEPTEMBER 2026

We ship nothing

Rubsta Open sells entry to a tennis tournament. There are no physical goods, no shipping and no delivery charges. Nothing is posted to you.

What you receive, and when

IMMEDIATELY
Your entry is marked paid on your account page as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment. That status is your receipt.
AFTER ENTRIES CLOSE
Draws and seedings are published on this website after 15 October 2026, 6:00 PM IST.
BEFORE PLAY
The order of play and your court and match times appear on this website once the schedule is set.
ON TOURNAMENT DAYS
Entry to the courts at Vazirani National Sports Academy on 24–25 October 2026, live scoring, and your player card.

If your entry does not show as paid

Sign in and open your entry page — the paid status there is the record that counts. If the entry still shows unpaid after a successful charge, email tech@forgelabs.in with the payment reference. Do not pay a second time.
