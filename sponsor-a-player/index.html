HOW WE CHOOSE

The player.
The attitude.
The effort.

We choose the player based on what they show on court:

Performance
How they perform and compete on court.
Conduct
How they carry themselves throughout a match.
Fighting spirit
The determination and effort they bring to the game.
Sportsmanship
The respect they show to opponents, officials and the game.
Discipline
The focus and self-control they show on court.

Ranking is not a selection criterion.
